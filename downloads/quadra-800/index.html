Quadra 800

make m68k builds the Macintosh Quadra 800 kernel (make run-m68k on QEMU q800).

Technical Characteristics of the Computer/Console

ChipsetMotorola 68040 @ 33 MHz (integrated MMU & FPU, Cleanroom µITRON 3.0 / BTRON 3.20)
Bus32-bit NuBus (3 slots + PDS), Apple Desktop Bus (ADB), MOS 6522 VIA1 / VIA2, NCR 53C96 ESP SCSI
VideoNuBus Slot 9 DAFB / MacFB linear framebuffer (src/drivers/m68k/)
Display800×600 @ 8-bpp indexed (DA-15 Apple display / QEMU q800)
SoundApple Sound Chip (EASC) 8-bit stereo PCM
InputADB keyboard & mouse via MOS 6522 VIA1 / VIA2 controllers
Memory8–136 MB 72-pin SIMM DRAM (128 MB under QEMU q800)
CommunicationsZilog Z8530 ESCC Dual UART (Port A active), SONIC 10 Mbps Ethernet (AAUI-15), LocalTalk
