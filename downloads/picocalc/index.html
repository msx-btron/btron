PicoCalc

UF2 firmware for RP2040 and RP2350.

make picocalc builds the PicoCalc images. Published files at the repository root include btron-picocalc-rp2040.uf2, btron-picocalc-rp2350.uf2, and the Quake and T-Kernel variants next to them. Sources: src/drivers/pico/.
