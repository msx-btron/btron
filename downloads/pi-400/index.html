Raspberry Pi 400

make pi400 builds btron-pi400.img; make flash-pi400 writes an SD card.

Technical Characteristics of the Computer/Console

ChipsetBroadcom BCM2711C0 (Quad-core ARM Cortex-A72 @ 1.8 GHz, AArch64, T-Kernel 2.0)
BusPCIe Gen 2 ×1 (pcie_bcm2711.c), VL805 xHCI USB 3.0 (xhci.c), DWC2 USB 2.0 (dwc2.c), BCM2711 DMA (bcm2711_dma.c)
VideoVideoCore VI (V3D 4.2 — v3d_init.c, v3d_cl.c, v3d_qpu.c) + Mailbox framebuffer
DisplayDual micro-HDMI; B-System LAPTOP shell (1280×1024 / 1024×768 / 800×600, 32-bpp)
SoundBCM2711 HDMI digital audio (bcm2711_hdmi_audio.c), USB Audio Class 1 (usb_audio_uac1.c)
InputIntegrated USB keyboard, USB HID mouse (xHCI / DWC2), Bluetooth HID
Memory4 GB LPDDR4-3200 SDRAM
CommunicationsGigabit Ethernet, Wi-Fi 802.11ac, Bluetooth 5.0 (src/drivers/bluetooth/), PL011 UART
