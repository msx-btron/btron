CLU

CLU · be / sc / sh / tv
Command language utilities: a binary editor, a sheet, a shell, and a viewer.
← Applications

Four faces

  • be — binary editor and disassembly, under src/apps/clu/be/.
  • sc — the sheet, under src/apps/clu/sc/.
  • sh — the shell, under src/apps/clu/sh/.
  • tv — the viewer, under src/apps/clu/tv/.

File verbs (cp, ls, mv, rm, ln) live next to them in src/apps/clu/.
