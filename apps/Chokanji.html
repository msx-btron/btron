Chokanji

超漢字 · B-right/V
The Cho-Kanji compatibility programs that sit beside the clean-room desk.
← Applications

What it is

Chokanji is the B-right/V Cho-Kanji layer: the personal media cabinet, the clock face, kana conversion, launchers, and the unpack path for a Cho-Kanji real body. The sources live in src/apps/chokanji/ (pmc.c, cab.c, kconv.c, launchers.c, unpack.c).

Beside the clean-room books

The TAD browser and the Cabinet read the same real bodies. Chokanji is the compatibility face of that desk, not a second document model.
